1. NORTH INDIA

KASHMIR, JAMMU AND KASHMIR

Famous Places: Dal Lake, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonamarg and Shankaracharya Temple.

Traditional Food: Rogan Josh, Kashmiri Pulao, Dum Aloo and Kahwa.

Traditional Dress: Pheran, a traditional loose garment, and Kashmiri embroidered clothing.

Culture and Festivals: Sufi music, Kashmiri handicrafts, saffron traditions and Tulip Festival.

Art and Shopping: Pashmina shawls, papier-mâché crafts and Kashmiri carpets.

JAIPUR, RAJASTHAN

Famous Places: Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort, City Palace, Jantar Mantar and Jal Mahal.

Traditional Food: Dal Baati Churma, Ghewar, Gatte Ki Sabzi and Laal Maas.

Traditional Dress: Ghagra Choli, Bandhani clothing, colourful turbans and traditional jewellery.

Culture and Festivals: Teej, Gangaur, Jaipur Literature Festival and traditional folk dances.

Art and Shopping: Blue pottery, block printing, miniature paintings and jewellery.

AGRA, UTTAR PRADESH

Traditional Dress: Sarees, salwar kameez, kurta-pajama and regional traditional clothing.

MANALI, HIMACHAL PRADESH

Famous Places: Solang Valley, Hadimba Temple, Old Manali and Rohtang Pass.

Traditional Food: Dham, Siddu, Madra and Babru.

Traditional Dress: Himachali caps, woollen shawls and regional dresses.

2. SOUTH INDIA

HAMPI, KARNATAKA

Famous Places: Virupaksha Temple, Vittala Temple, Stone Chariot, Lotus Mahal and Hemakuta Hill.

Traditional Food: Jolada Rotti, Ennegayi, Bisi Bele Bath and local Karnataka meals.

Traditional Dress: Ilkal sarees, traditional Karnataka sarees, Panche and Mysuru silk.

Culture and Festivals: Vijaya Utsav, traditional music, temple heritage and the history of the Vijayanagara Empire.

Art and Shopping: Stone carvings, local handicrafts and traditional textiles.

Explore: Famous Places | Food | Traditional Dress | Travel and Culture

COORG, KARNATAKA

Famous Places: Abbey Falls, Raja's Seat, Talakaveri and Dubare.

Traditional Food: Pandi Curry, Kadambuttu, Akki Rotti and Bamboo Shoot Curry.

Traditional Dress: Kodava traditional attire, including the distinctive Kodava sari and men's Kupya.

Culture and Festivals: Puthari harvest festival, Kailpodh and Kodava cultural traditions.

Art and Shopping: Coffee, spices, homemade chocolates and local crafts.

MYSURU, KARNATAKA

Traditional Food: Mysore Masala Dosa, Mysore Pak, Ragi Mudde and Mysore Bonda.

Traditional Dress: Mysore silk sarees, Panche and traditional Karnataka clothing.

Culture and Festivals: Mysuru Dasara, classical music, dance and royal heritage.

Art and Shopping: Mysore silk, sandalwood products, rosewood inlay and Mysore paintings.

6. NORTH EAST INDIA

MEGHALAYA

Famous Places: Cherrapunji, Dawki River, Living Root Bridges and Nohkalikai Falls.

Traditional Food: Jadoh, Dohneiiong, Tungrymbai and Pumaloi.

Traditional Dress: Jainsem and Dhara among Khasi communities, and other regional tribal attire.

Culture and Festivals: Wangala, Shad Suk Mynsiem and traditional Khasi and Garo dances.

Art and Shopping: Bamboo crafts, cane products and handwoven textiles.

Traditional Dress: Bakhu or Kho for different Sikkimese communities.

Culture and Festivals: Losar, Saga Dawa and Buddhist monastery traditions.

Famous Places: Radhanagar Beach, Cellular Jail, Neil Island and Ross Island.

Traditional Food: Fish curry, coconut-based dishes and fresh seafood.

Traditional Dress: Clothing traditions vary among the island communities. There is no single traditional outfit for all residents.

Culture and Festivals: Island community traditions, local festivals and maritime heritage.

Art and Shopping: Shell crafts, coconut crafts and local handicrafts.

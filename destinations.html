1. EUROPE

PARIS, FRANCE

Famous Places: Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, Palace of Versailles.

Traditional Food: Croissants, baguettes, ratatouille and macarons.

Traditional Clothing: France has regional traditional costumes, including Breton and Alsatian clothing.

Culture and Festivals: French art, fashion, music and Bastille Day celebrations.

SWITZERLAND

Famous Places: Matterhorn, Interlaken, Lucerne and Lake Geneva.

Traditional Food: Cheese fondue, raclette, rösti and Swiss chocolate.

Traditional Clothing: Regional folk costumes, including traditional dresses and embroidered outfits.

Culture and Festivals: Alpine traditions, music, yodelling and Swiss National Day.

Explore: Famous Places | Swiss Food | Traditional Dress | Travel and Culture

VENICE, ITALY

Famous Places: Grand Canal, Rialto Bridge and St. Mark's Square.

Traditional Food: Pizza, pasta, risotto and tiramisu.

Traditional Clothing: Italy has different regional folk costumes. Venetian Carnival costumes are especially famous.

Culture and Festivals: Venice Carnival, Italian art, architecture and music.

SANTORINI, GREECE

Famous Places: Oia Village, Fira, Red Beach and Akrotiri.

Traditional Food: Moussaka, souvlaki, Greek salad and baklava.

Traditional Clothing: Traditional Greek folk costumes include the fustanella and regional embroidered dresses.

Culture and Festivals: Greek music, dance, ancient history and religious celebrations.

Famous Places: Statue of Liberty, Times Square, Central Park and Empire State Building.

Popular Food: New York-style pizza, bagels, hot dogs and cheesecake.
